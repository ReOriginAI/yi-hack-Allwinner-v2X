RECOVERY FOR THIS CAMERA ONLY (@MODEL@)

Never use another camera's flash-core-recovery directory. Keep it and all
installer files: it holds private device identity, settings and original startup.

y623 needs FAT32 on SD partition 1 for U-Boot flashing (MBR recommended). FAT32
directly on a whole card without a partition table is unreadable to its updater.
If your old installation card uses that layout, preserve it and copy its entire
installation and flash-core-recovery/ directory to a compatible partitioned card
before requesting recovery. y28ga supports either FAT32 layout.

If hack_result.txt reports failure before images were staged, remove the SD
card with power OFF and boot again. The previous rootfs/home have not been
changed. Reclaimed updater files are backed up on SD. To undo staged writable
changes too, follow the full recovery below. Do not retry using a partially
prepared card without preserving its recovery directory.

To restore original rootfs/home and startup after this installer was activated:
1. Power OFF and put the SAME installation SD card in your computer.
2. Check flash-core-recovery/checksums.md5 from that directory with md5sum -c.
3. For a CLEAN REINSTALL (flash-core-recovery/clean.conf exists), use
   flash-core-recovery/files/rootfs.restore and home.restore. These restore the
   previous flash-core images, startup and internal settings. Do NOT use the
   bundled vendor images with a clean-reinstall recovery journal.
   For an original VENDOR INSTALL, copy flash-core/recovery/rootfs_@MODEL@.restore to the card root as
   rootfs_@MODEL@. Copy home_@MODEL@.restore there as home_@MODEL@.
4. Create an empty file flash-core-recovery/RESTORE (no extension).
5. Leave flash-core/ and flash-core-recovery/ intact. Insert the card and power
   on. Allow five minutes, stable power and automatic reboots. U-Boot restores
   rootfs/home; the dispatcher then verifies both originals and camera identity,
   restores original startup and reclaimed updater files, and retires triggers.
   hack_result.txt reports 'Original rootfs/home and startup restored'.
6. Power OFF, remove the card, and boot the previous firmware (previous
   flash-core after a clean reinstall, vendor after a vendor installation).

If the dispatcher was not activated yet, the installer SD hook also handles the
RESTORE marker once both stock images and recovery checksums match.
The compatible y623 VE kernel patch, if installed, is retained. Requested Wi-Fi
credentials are retained. This does not factory-reset the camera or erase its
identity. Do not write mtdblock4.bin (raw JFFS2), mfg, env or conf from generic
packages. Recovery requires a working U-Boot/SD path and cannot guarantee repair
of interrupted bootloader/kernel writes or faulty storage; UART may be needed.
