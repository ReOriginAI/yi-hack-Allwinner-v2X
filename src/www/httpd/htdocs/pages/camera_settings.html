Local Motion Detection

Motion detection Enable the local model-aware motion service. On y28ga this uses the firmware IVA detector and local IPC events. Vehicle/animal classifiers, face detection, and PTZ tracking remain disabled; optional human confirmation can be enabled below.
Human-confirmed motion only Y28GA only. Require the firmware human classifier to confirm motion before the hack publishes its local motion event. This filters local motion status plus MQTT/ONVIF motion consumers while generic motion still gates the IVA pipeline.
Foliage false-positive filter Y623 only. Require a stronger encoder-motion signature before publishing a local motion event. This is intended to reject low-level foliage/rustling-leaf motion without restoring the RAM-heavy PilotAI path.
